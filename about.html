02 — What we believe

Five beliefs that shape every engagement.

01

Evidence over opinion

If we can't show you the data, it isn't a recommendation — it's a hunch. Every change we make traces back to research or a live experiment, never a HiPPO in the room.

02

Measure to the sale, not the click

A test that lifts conversion but doesn't lift revenue hasn't won anything yet. We follow every result through to the actual outcome before we call it a win.

03

Report the losses too

Wins get remembered; losses get quietly buried, along with whatever they could have taught us. Every scorecard we produce shows what didn't work, right alongside what did.

04

Rigour over vanity tests

No peeking at significance early, no calling a test the moment it looks good, no statistics dressed up to justify a launch that was already decided.

05

Considered purchases deserve considered experimentation

A car, a home, a policy or a degree isn't an impulse buy. The research, the sample sizes and the patience all have to match the size of the decision you're actually influencing.
