02 — Why this matters

A “winning” test can still be losing you money.

Most measurement stops at the click, the add-to-cart, or a session recording someone half-watched: proxies that feel like progress but don't prove revenue actually moved. A PDP change that lifts add-to-cart 12% can still be flat, or worse, once you follow it through to completed checkout.

The questions above aren't a trick. They're the same checklist we use internally before we trust a single result. The lower your score, the more of your testing program is probably running on assumptions right now.
