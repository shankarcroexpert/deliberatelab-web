Experiment 001 Founding Shopify Plus client · Apparel & Accessories
Running
Shopify Plus PDP A/B Testing

Mobile PDP add-to-cart friction

Challenge
Strong mobile PDP traffic, but a soft add-to-cart rate compared to desktop.
Research finding
Session recordings showed shoppers repeatedly scrolling back to size and fit information before leaving the page.
Hypothesis
Surfacing size and fit guidance earlier, above the fold, will reduce mobile PDP abandonment before add-to-cart.
Experiment
A/B test: sticky mobile add-to-cart bar with inline size guidance vs. the current PDP layout.
Primary metricMobile add-to-cart rateATC
Secondary metricsPDP scroll depth, size-guide engagement

Experiment 002 Founding DTC client · Beauty & Personal Care
Running
Shopify Cart Checkout A/B Testing

Cart-to-checkout drop-off

Challenge
Cart-to-checkout completion running below category benchmarks.
Research finding
Voice-of-customer surveys pointed to shipping-cost surprise as the top cited reason for not completing checkout.
Hypothesis
Surfacing the free-shipping threshold earlier in the cart will reduce shipping-cost-driven abandonment.
Experiment
A/B test: a free-shipping progress bar on the cart page vs. the current cart.
Primary metricCart-to-checkout rateCTC
