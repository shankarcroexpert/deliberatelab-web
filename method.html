01 — How we measure

The loop that keeps every result honest.

Most CRO stops at the form. We don't. Every meaningful online action — a configurator session, a loan enquiry, a booked test drive — gets tied to what actually happens next: a delivery, a disbursement, a counter booking.

We agree the attribution window and matching method with you upfront, during the Audit, so by the time a test goes live, everyone already trusts the number it reports.

TEST DRIVE→delivery
LOAN ENQUIRY→disbursed amount
SITE VISIT→booking at the counter

Every scorecard reports the flat and losing tests too — not just the wins.

02 — Our process

Research. Prioritise. Experiment. Measure.

The same four-stage loop runs through every engagement, from the first Audit to the tenth month of a Program.

01

Research

We start in the field, not the dashboard — qualitative interviews, session recordings, on-site or in-showroom observation, and a review of your current analytics, to find where high-intent buyers actually hesitate.

  • Stakeholder and customer interviews
  • Session recordings and funnel analysis
  • Tracking and data-layer sanity check
02

Prioritise

Every candidate idea is scored on the PROOF framework — weighted so the biggest, most outcome-relevant bets rise to the top of the roadmap, instead of whatever's quickest to ship.

  • Scored against potential revenue, reach, odds of success, outcome impact and feasibility
  • Roadmap reviewed and agreed with you before a single test is built
03

Experiment

Controlled A/B tests where your volume supports them; disciplined, evidence-based rollouts where it doesn't — but never faked statistics or tests called early to hit a deadline.

  • Proper sample-size and duration planning up front
  • QA across desktop, mobile and your key browsers and devices
  • Rollouts watched against guardrail metrics, not just the primary one
04

Measure

Lift is tracked through to the actual outcome and reported in one honest scorecard — including the tests that lost or came back flat.

  • Online lift and lift at the sale reported side by side
  • Wins, losses and flat results — all of them, every time
  • Learnings folded back into the next round of research
