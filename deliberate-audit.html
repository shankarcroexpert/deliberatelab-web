02 — How an opportunity gets built

From a raw observation to a testable idea.

Every line on the roadmap goes through the same six steps before it earns a place on it.

Observation→ Evidence→ Hypothesis→ Change→ Test→ Learning

Worked example — illustrative, not a real client finding

Observation
Mobile PDP sessions have a high scroll depth but a low add-to-cart rate.
Evidence
Session recordings show shoppers repeatedly scrolling back to the size chart before leaving the page.
Hypothesis
Shoppers can't find sizing confidence fast enough, and give up before adding to cart.
Change
Move a condensed size guide inline, above the fold, next to the add-to-cart button.
Test
A/B test the inline size guide against the current PDP layout, read on add-to-cart rate.
Learning
Whatever the result, win, loss or flat, it tells us whether sizing confidence was really the blocker, sharpening the next hypothesis either way.
