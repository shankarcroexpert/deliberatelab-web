01 — The blind spot

The most important part of your funnel is the part you can’t see.

You spend to bring people to the site. But for a car, a home, a policy or a degree, the decision forms online and the purchase closes somewhere else — a showroom, a branch, a counsellor’s call.

Most agencies optimise the clicks and stop at the form. The revenue lives past the form, and that is exactly where measurement usually goes dark. You end up optimising the visible half of a journey whose outcome you can’t actually see.
